# Vloora public editorial example

Vloora's published editorials developed the Prosekeep method. This abridged example explains the configuration; the actual owner-approved instructions live in Vloora's repository and remain authoritative.

## Page responsibilities

Home establishes the company field and point of view. Approach explains the mechanisms. Products identifies working tools and their actual status. Product pages demonstrate one real capability. Essays preserve the author's voice and attributed opinions. Technical notes describe states and evidence. Research notes and discussion papers preserve methods and citations; news records events with dates.

## Writing and review

Use plain, confident sentences about recognizable circumstances. Avoid formulaic contrast, staged importance, artificial triads, vague headings and repeated slogans. Preserve relevant facts, names, chronology, sources, and genuine uncertainty. Run source checks; verify claims against the actual product or fixture. Vloora's chosen mechanical conventions include month-first prose dates and a no-prose-dashes preference; the `vloora` profile enables those checks.

## Precedence

This example is illustrative, not a replacement for Vloora's full internal manual, `AGENTS.md`, approved positioning, site generator, Vale config or human review process. The project's latest explicit owner instruction governs.
